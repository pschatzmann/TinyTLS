Detailed Description

ChaCha20-Poly1305 authenticated encryption (RFC 8439 section 2.8).

The AEAD construction used by TLS_CHACHA20_POLY1305_SHA256. The one-time Poly1305 key comes from the first ChaCha20 block, and the payload is encrypted from block counter 1. Nonces are 12 bytes and tags are 16 bytes.

encrypt() and decrypt() work in place. decrypt() verifies the tag in constant time before it produces plaintext, and zeros the output on failure.

Never reuse a (key, nonce) pair: that breaks both confidentiality and the authenticator.

ChaCha20Poly1305 aead;
aead.setKey(key);
aead.encrypt(nonce, aad, aad_len, plain, n, cipher, tag);
Error rc = aead.decrypt(nonce, aad, aad_len, cipher, n, tag, plain);
