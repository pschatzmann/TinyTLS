Detailed Description

Public key taken from an X.509 certificate.

The key is ECDSA on P-256 (an uncompressed point) or RSA. parse() reads the content of a SubjectPublicKeyInfo structure and rejects other curves and algorithms. verify() checks a signature over a message with the scheme codes that TLS 1.3 uses in CertificateVerify and in certificate signatures:

  • 0x0403 ECDSA with SHA-256 (DER-encoded signature),
  • 0x0401 RSASSA-PKCS1-v1_5 with SHA-256,
  • 0x0804 RSASSA-PSS with SHA-256.

A scheme that does not match the key type fails, so schemes cannot be mixed up.
