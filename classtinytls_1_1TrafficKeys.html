Detailed Description

AEAD keys for one direction of the TLS 1.3 record layer.

install() derives the key and IV from a traffic secret and starts a fresh record sequence. seal() and open() protect records in place; the nonce is the IV XORed with the 64-bit sequence number. The class is internal to the session.

The current secret is kept so that KeyUpdate can derive the next one. The key material is wiped on reset() and on destruction.
