Detailed Description

X.509 certificates (RFC 5280 subset): DER parsing, PEM decoding, trust stores and chain verification for TLS 1.3 server authentication.

Supported: ECDSA P-256 keys with ecdsa-with-SHA256, RSA keys up to TINYTLS_MPI_MAX_LIMBS limbs with sha256WithRSAEncryption and RSASSA-PSS (SHA-256, MGF1, salt 32). Hostnames are matched against dNSName entries of subjectAltName only. Issuer and subject are compared as DER encodings.
